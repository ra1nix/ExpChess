# ExpChess
When teaching kids chess, i noticed the lack of understanding the positions and only repeating what they have faced previously, therefore i came with the idea of Expchess.

Expchess is a simple, self explainable chess engine that is created to encounter the blind memorization of the moves, and instead build a strong and logical comprehension of the positions and the best moves.
that is the prototype (v1.0) of my project
using python for the logic, principals and explanations & HTML for the visuals.

## what is featured:
-the principals of the game
-visual play board
-freedom to play either black or white
-explanation of the bot's best moves

## future coming improvements:
