# ExpChess
When teaching kids chess i noticed the lack of understanding the positions and only repeating what they have encounterd before, thereore i came with the idea of Expchess

Expchess is a simple, self explainable chess engine that is created to encounter the blind memorizing of the moves and instead build a strong and logical comprehension the the position and the best moves

that is the prototype (v1.0) for my project
using python for the logic, principals and explanations & HTML for the visuals

## what is featured:
-the principals of the game
-visual play board
-freedom to play either black or white and
-explanation of the bot's best moves

## future coming improvements:
-option to highlight the piece's legal moves
-adding more chess concepts
